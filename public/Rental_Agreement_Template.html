3. Condition and Return of Trailer

You must return the Trailer on the date and time specified in this Agreement, to the location where you rented it or another location we specify, in the same condition that you received it except for ordinary wear. If you return the Trailer to a location other than the location where you rented it, you will pay our one-way service charge. Service charges are calculated based on our experience that the costs of repositioning the Trailer and the lost rental revenue are higher for one-way rentals than for rentals where the Trailer is returned to the location from which it is rented. Late returns incur a fee of $50 per hour.

You are responsible for all damage to, or loss or theft of, the Trailer, whether or not you are at fault. You must report all accidents or incidents of theft or vandalism to us and the police within 24 hours of occurrence.

5. Prohibited Uses

The following uses are prohibited and a violation of this Agreement:

  • Transporting dangerous or hazardous materials, contraband, or persons for hire
  • Transporting items prohibited by law
  • Use by any person who is under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Use by any person whose driver's license is suspended
  • Loading the Trailer beyond its rated capacity or overloading
  • Pushing or towing anything
  • Use in furtherance of any illegal purpose or under any circumstance that would constitute a felony or other violation of law
  • Intentional or reckless damage to the Trailer

6. Cancellation & Refund Policy

IMPORTANT CANCELLATION POLICY:

All cancellations must be made through your customer portal at molallatrailerrental.com.

  • More than 24 hours before rental start: 100% refund of rental fee (deposit is non-refundable)
  • 12-24 hours before rental start: 50% refund of rental fee (deposit is non-refundable)
  • Less than 12 hours before rental start: NO REFUND

The $50 deposit is always non-refundable upon cancellation. For completed rentals, the deposit will be refunded only after trailer inspection confirms it is returned in good, clean condition.
